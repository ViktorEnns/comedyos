## Quick Reference Summary

- **Design Philosophy:** "The Typographic Ledger" & "Gallery Editorial" — the interface is the gallery, content is the exhibit. Modeled after a literary monograph and precision ledger, not a SaaS dashboard.
- **Canvas:** Matte warm charcoal (`#1A1918`).
- **Typography:**
  - **Headlines / Display:** `Newsreader` / `Crimson Pro` (Serif Display, light weights 300–400)
  - **Body / Metadata:** `Inter` (Sans, 300–500)
  - **Telemetry / Timecodes:** `JetBrains Mono` (Monospace, tabular figures)
  - **Labels:** `all-small-caps`, `letter-spacing: 0.16em` to `0.20em`, tracking uppercase
- **Hierarchy:** Solved via **typography, contrast, and whitespace** — never via 4-sided boxed cards, dropshadows, or nested container borders.
- **Buttons & Interactive Controls:** Crisp tactile affordance (`1px solid` hairline stroke, `2px` micro-chamfer radius, uppercase tracking). Never unstyled plain text, never flat pastel slabs.
