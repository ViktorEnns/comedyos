Getting Started

An orientation guide for navigating the verbatim records of modern comedy masterclasses.

2.1

The Dataset

The ComedyOS Archive consists of exactly seventy verbatim quotes curated from seven distinct podcast masterclasses. These entries represent the atomic units of stand-up comedy knowledge, captured in their original syntax to preserve the speaker's cadence and intent.

Each entry is cataloged with a precise timestamp and source attribution. Use this resource not as a narrative, but as a typographic ledger for studying the mechanics of humor.
