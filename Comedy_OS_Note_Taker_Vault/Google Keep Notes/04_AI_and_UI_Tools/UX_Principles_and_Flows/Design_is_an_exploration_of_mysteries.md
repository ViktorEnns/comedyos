# Design is an exploration of mysteries

Design is a continuing exploration of mysteries, not a collection of firmly established truths.

The public has a distorted view of design, because people assume that design is a collection of firmly established truths. In fact, design is not a collection of truths. It is a continuing exploration of mysteries.  

- via Freeman Dyson
