# Beginner Yoga Flow

## Variant 1: Beginner Yoga Flow `(2026-04-04 11:16)`
*Source File: `Beginner Yoga Flow(1).json`*

Part 1: Warm-Up & Initial Side Stretch

1.  Easy Pose (Sukhasana): Sit comfortably cross-legged. Rest your hands on your knees, close your eyes, and take 5 deep breaths in and out through your nose.
2.  Seated Side Bend (Side Torso): Keep your right hand on the floor beside your right hip. Inhale and reach your left arm up toward the ceiling. Exhale and gently lean to the right, feeling a stretch along your left ribcage. Keep both hips grounded. Hold for 3 breaths, then switch sides.
3.  Cat-Cow: Come onto all fours (hands under shoulders, knees under hips). Inhale (Cow): Drop your belly, lift your chest and tailbone. Exhale (Cat): Round your spine toward the ceiling and tuck your chin. Repeat for 5 rounds to warm up the spine.

Part 2: Sun Salutation A (Surya Namaskar A)
Complete 3 rounds of this sequence, moving one breath per movement.

1.  Mountain Pose (Tadasana): Stand at the top of your mat, feet hip-width apart, arms at your sides.
2.  Upward Salute: Inhale, reach your arms up and over your head.
3.  Forward Fold: Exhale, hinge at your hips and fold forward. Keep a generous bend in your knees so your hands can touch your shins or the floor.
4.  Halfway Lift: Inhale, slide your hands up your shins, straighten your legs slightly, and flatten your back so your torso is parallel to the floor.
5.  Plank Pose: Exhale, plant your hands and step both feet back. Beginner modification: Drop your knees to the mat.
6.  Lower Down: Exhale, keeping your elbows tucked close to your ribs, lower your body all the way down to your belly.
7.  Baby Cobra: Inhale, press the tops of your feet into the mat and gently lift your chest off the floor using your back muscles.
8.  Downward-Facing Dog: Exhale, tuck your toes, press into your hands, and lift your hips up and back. Keep your knees slightly bent. Hold here for 3 deep breaths.
9.  Step Forward: Look between your hands and step your feet to the top of the mat.
10. Halfway Lift: Inhale, flatten your back.
11. Forward Fold: Exhale, fold over your legs.
12. Upward Salute: Inhale, press into your feet and sweep your arms all the way up.
13. Mountain Pose: Exhale, bring your hands to your sides.

Part 3: Sun Salutation B (Surya Namaskar B)
Complete 2 rounds.

1.  Chair Pose: Inhale, bend your knees and sink your hips back as if sitting in a chair. Reach your arms up.
2.  Forward Fold: Exhale, straighten your legs slightly and fold forward.
3.  Halfway Lift: Inhale, flatten your back.
4.  Plank to Belly: Exhale, step back to plank and lower all the way to your belly.
5.  Baby Cobra: Inhale, lift your chest.
6.  Downward-Facing Dog: Exhale, lift your hips up and back.
7.  Warrior I (Right): Inhale, step your right foot forward between your hands. Spin your left heel down to the mat at a 45-degree angle. Bend your right knee and reach both arms up to the ceiling.
8.  Plank to Belly: Exhale, bring your hands back to the mat, step your right foot back, and lower down.
9.  Baby Cobra: Inhale, lift your chest.
10. Downward-Facing Dog: Exhale, lift your hips.
11. Warrior I (Left): Inhale, step your left foot forward, spin the right heel down, and reach your arms up.
12. Plank to Belly: Exhale, hands down, step back, lower down.
13. Baby Cobra: Inhale, lift your chest.
14. Downward-Facing Dog: Exhale, lift your hips. Hold here for 3 deep breaths.
15. Step Forward: Walk your feet to the top of the mat.
16. Halfway Lift: Inhale, flatten your back.
17. Forward Fold: Exhale, fold down.
18. Chair Pose: Inhale, bend your knees, drop your hips, and reach your arms up.
19. Mountain Pose: Exhale, stand up straight and bring your arms to your sides.

Part 4: Deep Side Torso Stretches

1.  Standing Crescent Moon: From Mountain Pose, interlace your fingers overhead. Inhale to get tall, then Exhale and gently bump your hips to the left while reaching your upper body to the right. Hold for 3-5 breaths. Switch sides.
2.  Gate Pose (Parighasana): Come down to a kneeling position. Extend your right leg straight out to the side. Inhale, reach your left arm up. Exhale, slide your right hand down your right leg and lean your torso to the right. Hold for 5 slow breaths. Return to center and switch legs.

Part 5: Cool Down

1.  Child's Pose (Balasana): Bring your knees wide on the mat, big toes touching. Sit your hips back onto your heels and walk your hands forward, resting your forehead on the mat. Take 5-10 deep breaths.
2.  Corpse Pose (Savasana): Roll over onto your back. Extend your legs out straight and let your feet flop open. Rest your arms by your sides with your palms facing up. Close your eyes and allow your breathing to normal. Rest here for 3 to 5 minutes.
