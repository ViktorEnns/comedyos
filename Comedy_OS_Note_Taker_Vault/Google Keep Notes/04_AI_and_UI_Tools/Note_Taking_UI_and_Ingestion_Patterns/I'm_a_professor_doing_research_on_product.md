Develop ideas for an innovative and effective suitcase design for adult men. Think about what these men want or need in a suitcase. Consider the features or benefits that might need to be included so that they are interested in buying this suitcase.

As you brainstorm your design, consider the following:
Use your imagination; picture the suitcase in your mind. Use imagery that goes beyond suitcases you would normally see.
Visualize your potential customer. Seeing in your mind an adult man being involved with and interacting with the product can help to develop an effective design.
Do not overthink or criticize your ideas. Write them down and keep going.
Disregard any economic, material, or regulatory constraints as you develop your ideas.
Finally, please do not search online for any suitcase products while you are doing this survey. Please do not use AI or any other software to generate ideas for this suitcase. We want to know YOUR ideas, and your ideas alone.
Remember: Our research finds that men strongly preferred products that were high in functional value. 
 
My Ideas 

A rolling suitcase. Sturdy but light. It has very handles on all sides to accommodate various varying scenarios. 

Inside it has modular compartments that can be closed and taken out separately. Nothing flies around or falls out. They are flat bags, light but sore everything well and cleanly.

The long rolling handle doesn't wobble and accommodates tall men. The wheels are constructed in a way that they make minimal noise and do not get stuck in small crevasses. 

Based on weight there's a mechanism that jumps the suitcase up when a button on the handle is pressed to give a "running start" when lifting it above your head. Sometimes you need it to jump to lift it up higher using the jump momentum. 

It has a built in scale to know how heavy it is exactly
