# user guidelines in community (notebooklm discord)

Be mature. No hate speech or trolling. We have zero tolerance for homophobic, racist, sexist, transphobic, and other discriminatory remarks and slurs.

Be appropriate. No nsfw content in any form. Including text, images, memes, usernames, profile photos and gifs.

Be calm. Do not misuse or spam channels. No long passages of text, excessive emoji use, excessive gif use, irrelevant content, or anything that will disrupt the flow of discussion.

Avoid discussion of sensitive topics. Keep conversations appropriate for all ages. We want this to be a safe place for every member of the community.

Avoid advertising.
