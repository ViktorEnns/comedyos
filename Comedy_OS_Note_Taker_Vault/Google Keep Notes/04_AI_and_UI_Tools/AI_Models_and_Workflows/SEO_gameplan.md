# SEO gameplan

Scan old content for improvement possibilities 
Catalogue old content
Create an AI tool that suggests changes to improve old and new content based on changes in the SEO and LLM functionality
Monitor content visibility daily
Pull declining content into the improvement pipeline immediately
Work beyond your main domain: Reddit, quota, other social media, Medium, LinkedIn, industry forums—anywhere LLMs might discover your expertise
