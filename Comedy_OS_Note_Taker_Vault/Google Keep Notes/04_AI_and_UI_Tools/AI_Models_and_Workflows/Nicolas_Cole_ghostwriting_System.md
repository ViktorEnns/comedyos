# Nicolas Cole ghostwriting System

> [!INFO] Cross-Vault Architecture Connections
> **Related System Modules:** [[31_Visual_Diff_Report]]

In the last 5 years, my little business has trained 12,000+ writers, generated over $19,000,000, and grown to a team of 30 all-stars.

Building digital businesses isn't complicated.                    

Here's the simple system I use to turn any idea into a 7-figure business:

1. Understand the real bottleneck.

When I co-founded a cohort-based, 30-day writing challenge (Ship 30 for 30), we didn’t build it to "teach writing."

It was built to get people writing and publishing daily for 30 days.

People wanted transformation—not reams of endless information.

So, that’s what we delivered every single day of the cohort.

2. Prove your ideas work before building a product.

Before Ship 30 became a 7-figure course, I manually coached 100+ people.

If you can't help 1 person get results, you won't help 1,000.

Your product is only as good as your ability to solve real problems.

So skip funnels & tech.

Instead, start with coaching calls:

• Teach what you know
• Learn what your ideal client needs
• Iterate to improve the product over and over again

Because fast feedback builds both the skill of teaching and the clarity on what you need to teach.

3. Don’t structure the course around information.

People don’t want to learn.

They want results:

• Where’s the student starting?
• Where do they want to go?
• What are the steps to get them there?

Teach them only what they need to do.

4. Build a traffic engine.

Courses don’t sell themselves. You need consistent attention.

There are only 2 types of traffic:

• Paid (risky early on)
• Organic (trust compounding)

Start organic.

Then scale with paid.

5. Funnel 101: Prove you can help, first.

We used to send people straight to a sales page.

Then we built a free email course.

This had the same traffic but a different email sequence.

The result?

The business tripled.

Funnels are just conversations at scale, which builds trust, making it easier for people to buy from you.

6. Front-load the value.

That’s the whole game.

We stopped sending people our “latest” newsletter, and started sending them our 10 best.

Why?

Because your funnel should provide value immediately.

7. Get comfortable with boring.

The biggest competitive edge isn’t talent. It’s reps.

I’ve rewritten our curriculum and sales sequence dozens of times.

Millions of words.

The more boring the work, the higher the wall you're building around your business.

8. Evolve the model.

Ship 30 used a cohort model—great for FOMO, but feedback loops were slow.

So we built a group coaching program, Premium Ghostwriting Academy:

• Live cohorts: 90-day feedback
• Group coaching: feedback in hours

The faster the loop, the faster you grow.

And that’s how we built Ship 30 from a free Slack channel to $2.5M+ in revenue in 12 months.

We mentored 10,000+ writers and helped them publish every day for 30 days. We gamified their learning. And built a global community in the process.

This writing course became the industry standard.
