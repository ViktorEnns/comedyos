Premise: roasting is fun. Being roasted not so much. especially when you're being roasted by non comedians who tend to insult more than roast. They don't know how to go about writing jokes much less roast jokes. We help them navigate the intricacies of roasting without insulting too much.

So the idea is to have roast parties enabled by an app that gives them a structure and makes roasting into a game. 

Prep: roasters write down everything they know about the roastee and themselves. If necessary they interview them and their friends through the app, all the information gets thrown into a roast pot and everyone who roasts has the same starting point plus a wild card (some unique, hidden information).

Enter pro comedians: roasters can enlist professionals for a fee to help them punch up jokes or get guidance. Sort of an Uber for comedy newbies \ visitors. 

Imagine a pub quiz type of situation but with dick jokes instead of world knowledge.

goals of roasting:

show / find out more about a person
be mean but respectful
show Genuine Affection Beneath the Insults 
overarching pattern of roasts is a façade of extreme hostility masking genuine affection 
find the flip - first examine the person's actual status and then flip it upside down. if somebody is generous and helpful show how they have been greedy before.




possible areas of roasting (attack points): 
- personality (what do people say about this person in general? do patterns appear? how is this person perceived by others?)
- physical appearance (weight, facial expression, height, visual curiosities like long limbs etc)
- recent achievements / developments
- opposite day - take a positive trait like tireless worker and flip it to something negative 
- take one small detail about the person and blow it up to godzilla level hyperbole
- claim the opposite to show the actual truth
- do not make up stuff, that is easily verifiable. It looks cheap.
- note your own status in comparison to the other person (you're an outsider, you are established and they are not, you are serious and they are a clown etc)
- state how you are a close friend and then reveal a detail only a true friend would know about somthing everybody knows already about this person.
-  we are such good friends - here are the scars to prove it
