1. On the Core Problem & Your Solution's "Hook"

write, perform, analyze, innovate

help with the writing process and inspirational wisdom from the best comedians in the world.

the set building aspect and the audience reactions are a big help where you can see big reactions instantly instead of listening to endless recordings of your own voice. most people have a hard time hearing their own voice.

2. On Your Go-To-Market & Early Traction

not called any comedians yet but once i have a working prototype the goal is to reach out to ari matti of kill tony fame, hans kim (who is afaik very meticulous with digital notes and would probably appreciate a note taking app tailored to his needs). another big name is kevin smith from king of the hill who is constantly searching for a good note taking app.  if they like it i would like to get their endorsements. another name whom i personally know is nigel ng (uncle roger). i would reach out to him to ask for his input. 
- mark normand uses pieces of paper he is in danger of always losing. i would try and ask him to try digitizing the notes to the app and see if his workflow improves. 

another name is jimmy carr who is constantly writing and on tour. afaik he works digitally and would possibly be interested. if he is interested and likes it that would be a big get. he is also working on a book teaching comedy which would be a great tie into the app. 


3. On Your Defensible Moat

i am in the german market primarily. it is small and not well discovered by US companies yet. my goal is to grow the app and help the german comedy market grow at the same time. maybe integrating or building marketing tools like upnex or something along similar tools.

my competitive advantage is that i am small in a small market, invisible and nimble. i can move, adapt and grow much faster. essentially i can explode out of nothing over night before the big dogs can react. sort of like figma did in the ux design market. that gives me a strategic advantage over established brands. 

comedy OS (operating system) - the app / service i am building is in a corner that has not been tapped yet. it is a full service comedy writing system that is as powerful as you need it to be. think of google workspace and google tools for comedy. 

4. On the Team & Founder-Market Fit

currently i am the only one on the team. i have been a comedy nerd for over 35 years. without being a comedian i am extremely comedy literate and understand joke writing on an intimate level. i write jokes all the time and have a natural feeling for "funny" it is not just my opinion. it has been confirmed countless times. give me a minute and i can make you laugh. 

this deep understanding of funny is a huge advantage over people who are not as passionate about comedy. 

my current disadvantage in germany is that i dislike german comedy and german comedians. but maybe with the help of comedy os the market can become better and i will start to like it more if i dive deeper into it. and i will discover great talent. 

i also have ux design and ux research experience. i am very strong at intervieweing people and at finding problems in the workflow that need to be addressed to make an app more intuitive. i have built multiple apps by myself with the help of AI tools. i plan to build the initial prototype of comedy OS myself too. actually i already have a version built that is quite rudimentary but has some interesting features like:

- non linear timeline editing for a set. 
- a node based editor sort of like a diagramming tool like miro or figjam to sort your jokes inside a set to understand relationships. 
- tagging system

the new app will include features like:

- performance recording and audience reaction tracking and rating
- performance mode that helps you stay on track but adapt your set intuitively

a little bit more about me:

i also have a lot of experience working in teams as a team lead and project owner. i used to be a photographer and i organised, planned and ran shoots. i did live events where timing and precision are paramount. i know how to work well under pressure and be calm working with others when things do not go well. 


5. On Your Business Model & Unit Economics

- limit recording sets to 5 minutes
- ai features behind paywall
- 30 day free premium with a 1 set per day requirement which promises to improve the comic's performance within a month. once subscribed the streak keeps going and rewards with additional points for longer streaks. 

i am not sure about target conversion rates or a specific data driven strategy. i need help with that. i have no business plan only business wishes. 

becoming a free tool charity in the beginning may be necessary to bind users to the platform and get the name out there. figma did it and it worked. google does it and it works. i am sure a monetizing scheme will present itself once the app is in circulations.

currently i do not care about the app making money but i am convinced it will. my goal is to make a great product.

i am very good at thinking of how to monetize stuff though so i am not worried about finding a fair monetization scheme along the way. 

6. On the Big Vision & The Exit

since the german market is basically untapped and most german event apps and sites are horrendous from a usability perspective i can see the app expanding into live events in general. especially teaching newcomers about how to earn money with their talent. 

my endgame is to grow with the booming comedy market first. then it is to expand into a chain of german comedy clubs booking live shows and comedy each night. find great talent and raise them up to be stars. start with once a month in one location (saarbrücken) and grow into more and more. 

the endgame would be to make saarbrücken into german comedy mekka like austin texas has become in recent years with kill tony.
