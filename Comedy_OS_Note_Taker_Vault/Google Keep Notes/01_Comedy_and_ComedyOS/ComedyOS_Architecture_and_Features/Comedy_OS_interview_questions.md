# Comedy OS interview questions

Comedy OS interview questions

Build an interview chat bot that will also comedians questions about their process.

Do you use anything to help you write? 
What kind of tools do you use to write? 
How do you write? What does your prices look like? 
When do you write? 
When do you feel the most comfortable writing? 
When do you want to write but can't fit some reason? 
What does the ideal writing process look like to you?
Have you ever had a great writing session? What did it look like? 

What blocks you from writing?
What would be the worst way to write for you? 

If there was a perfect way to write what would it look like? 

If there was a tool to measure the success of a joke would you be interested in testing it?


Do you record your sets? 
Do you listen to them? Why? If not, Why not? 
What are you listening for? 
Do you make notes about the audience when performing? What do they look like? 

How do you organise your jokes, sets?
How do you measure the success of a joke or a set? 
Do you count the laughs you get from a joke? 

Do you have a ritual for writing? What does it look like?
