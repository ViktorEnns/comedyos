# Writing & Process

Writing & Process
Tools & Process: What gadgets, apps, or methods do you use to write your material? Walk me through the whole workflow.
Timing: When do you write? When does it feel easy? When do you want to write but can't?
Idea Capture: When a funny idea hits you on the street, what's the fastest way you can currently save it before you lose it? Do you use any notes apps?
Roadblocks: What's the main thing that stops you from writing?
Rituals: Do you have any weird or specific routines or rituals before a writing session?
Ideal State: What does the absolute best, most comfortable, most effective writing process look like to you? What would the writing process look like if you could snap your fingers and magically write?
For Organization & Editing
Techie: Would you describe yourself as a tech savvy person?
Organization: How do you organize your jokes and set lists?
"Dead" Jokes: How do you decide when a joke is officially "retired" or "killed"? Do you need a way to visually tag jokes with a status like "Active," "Testing," or "Bomb Log"?
Joke Connection: When you're building a set, how do you decide which joke should follow another?
Refining Text: When you're editing your jokes, do you ever wish you had a tool to instantly make them shorter or find a less common word? How much do you focus on syllable count or word economy?
