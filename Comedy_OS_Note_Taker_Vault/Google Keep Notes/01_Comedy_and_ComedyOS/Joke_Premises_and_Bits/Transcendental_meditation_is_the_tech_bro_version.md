# Transcendental meditation is the tech bro version

Transcendental meditation is the tech bro version of taking a nap. 

Oh I can't lay down. Laying down is weak. I'm a strong alpha male I need to sit upright, close my eyes and pretend to sleep for 25 minutes. If my shareholders come through the door. I can always say I'm meditating. And they'll be like: of he's so organized. He's not lazy at all. But if I lay down for 25 minutes and wake up refreshed and work for 4 more hours with a clear head I'm a lazy prick wasting company money.
