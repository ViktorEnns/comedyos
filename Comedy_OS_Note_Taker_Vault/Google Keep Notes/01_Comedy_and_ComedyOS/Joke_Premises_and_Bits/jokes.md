How hell works: 
You do something 'bad'. 
You die. 
You get judged. 
You get sent to hell. 

What happens there? 
You're supposed to feel bad for doing the bad thing. 
The devil tortures you for eternity so you feel bad for the thing you did. 

Assumptions
After some time you'll start to forget the bad thing you did. You'll start to forget who you were and who your victim was. The pain is just pain at that point. Nothing to learn from it.

Questions
You need a body to feel pain. Does your body exist in heaven? If not do you get a separate body for your spirit? 
Who's body is it? Was it cloned? Does good have cloning technology?
