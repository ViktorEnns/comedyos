# 💡 Concept + 💎 Value = 💹

💡 Concept + 💎 Value = 💹 Sales

The iPod was not the first, or even the best, MP3 player.

But it did have the strongest message:

"1,000 songs in your pocket."

That message got people to understand What It Is and Why It's Good.

💡 Concept (what it is)
Portable device that plays digital music files.

💎 Value (why it's good)
I can listen to way more music, without needing to carry CDs around.



Your buyer must understand both before they'll buy from you.
