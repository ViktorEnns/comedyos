# your perspective is your product - alex james

A mildly evil question I like to ask founders:

Imagine you're about to walk on stage and deliver a talk in front of 1,000 of your best-fit prospects.

They've never heard of you.

The host opens his mouth to introduce you.

What should he say?

...

If you're fuzzy on your answer, you're not alone.

Most founders are.
Some panic at the thought.

But it's not a confidence problem.

It's a clarity problem.

It's a problem of knowing so much you lose sight of what you should be known for.

And this is another way having a perspective makes life easier.

Because when you have a clear argument against how things are typically done in your industry, and for how they should be, it stops being about you.

No more resume read-out, awards rattle off, vibes and values noise.

Instead, you become associated with an argument for how things ought to be.

And that's way more interesting.
Way more repeatable.
Way more ownable.

That's what you should be known for.

Your perspective is your product.
