# Carbonara recipe

Ingredients

Egg yolk, 2x per person
Guanciale
Speck (smoked, unsmoked) or Pancetta
Pecorino Romano
Parmigiano Regiano or Grana Padano, older is better, at least 16 months 
Bay leafs 
Pepper black, tea spoon for 2 people
Pepper white, 2 grains per person 
Salt
Pasta, molisana, rummo, dececco, 


Tools 

Mortar pestle
Whisk
