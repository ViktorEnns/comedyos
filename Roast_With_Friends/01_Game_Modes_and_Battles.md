# 🎮 Roast with Friends: Game Modes & Battle Formats

Roast with Friends offers 4 distinct game modes tailored for informal green rooms, kitchen tables, or full stage events:

---

## 1. ⚔️ The Classic 1v1 Roast Battle
* **Structure:** 3 alternating rounds between two competitors.
* **Timing:**
  - Round 1: 45 seconds per comic (Warm-up burns)
  - Round 2: 45 seconds per comic (Deep cuts & rebuttals)
  - Round 3: 60 seconds per comic (Closing knockout jokes)
* **Rebuttal Window:** If Comic B naturally riffs an unwritten comeback to Comic A’s preceding joke, the acoustic referee awards a **+15% "Instant Rebuttal" Multiplier** if the room erupts.

---

## 2. ⭕ The Roast Cypher (3 to 6 Players)
* **Structure:** Circular free-for-all for friend groups.
* **Mechanics:**
  - The app spins a digital wheel or selects a "Hot Seat" target for 90 seconds.
  - All other players have 30 seconds each to deliver their best burn targeted at the player in the hot seat.
  - Hot seat rotates clockwise until all players have survived.
  - Winner is the player who accumulated the highest laugh yield across all rounds.

---

## 3. ⏱️ The Topic Sprint / Premise Roulette
* **Structure:** Focuses on pure speed-writing and spontaneous joke architecture.
* **Mechanics:**
  - The system generates an unexpected topic (e.g., *"Worst Airbnb Host"*, *"Cryptocurrency Bros"*, or a bizarre trending news headline).
  - Both players have **3 minutes on a silent countdown clock** to write 2 punchlines.
  - Comics perform their punchlines back-to-back.
  - Demonstrates raw writing agility under pressure.

---

## 4. 🥊 The Punch-Up Shootout (Cooperative / Competitive)
* **Structure:** Turning joke weakness into a high-stakes challenge.
* **Mechanics:**
  - Comic A reads a setup from their [[Comedy_OS_Note_Taker_Vault/40_Atomic_Bit_Database|Atomic Bit Database]] that currently has a flat or B-level punchline.
  - Competitors have 60 seconds to pitch the funniest alternate tag or punchline.
  - Comic A and the room rate the best tag.
  - The winner earns ELO points and is credited as a co-author if Comic A saves it.
