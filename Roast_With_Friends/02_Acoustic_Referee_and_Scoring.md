# 🤖 Acoustic Referee & Objective Scoring Algorithm

In human-judged roast battles, bias, grudges, and subjective friendships often derail the decision. Roast with Friends introduces the **Acoustic Referee**, an automated judge powered by Comedy OS audio intelligence.

---

## 📐 The Laugh Energy Formula

The Acoustic Referee calculates the **Laugh Energy ($LE$)** generated by each competitor's round:

$$LE = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( \text{Duration}_i \times 10^{\frac{\text{Peak\_dB}_i - \text{Floor\_dB}}{20}} \right)$$

Where:
* $N$ = Number of distinct audience laugh bursts during and immediately after the turn.
* $\text{Duration}_i$ = Seconds of sustained crowd audio exceeding the room baseline threshold (> 300ms).
* $\text{Peak\_dB}_i$ = Peak decibel amplitude of the reaction roar.
* $\text{Floor\_dB}$ = Ambient room noise floor calibrated 10 seconds before the match starts.

---

## 🛡️ Anti-Cheating & Voice Discrimination Filters

1. **Self-Laughter & Mic Screaming Penalty:**
   - If a comic yells into the mic or excessively laughs at their own joke to pump the volume meter, the bandpass filter isolates vocal formants (200Hz–3.5kHz) from diffuse crowd reverberation (500Hz–8kHz).
   - Audio energy originating within 10cm of the primary microphone is filtered out of the crowd score.
2. **Clapter / Polite Chuckle Demotion:**
   - Reactions with rapid decay (< 1.2s) or low decibel variance are categorized as "polite chuckles" and weighted at $0.3\times$.
   - True guttural belly laughs (> 2.5s, multi-frequency burst) receive full $1.0\times$ credit.
3. **Dead Air Flatline Penalty:**
   - Silence valleys exceeding 4.0 seconds without speech reduce the round score by $-10\%$.

---

## 🗳️ Hybrid Tie-Breakers: Peer Pulse Voting

If two comics finish within $\pm 3\%$ of each other's Laugh Energy score:
* The screen displays an **Overtime / Peer Pulse Tiebreaker**.
* Spectators can scan a room QR code or tap a 2-button screen card (`[Comic A]` or `[Comic B]`) with 10 seconds to decide the winner.
