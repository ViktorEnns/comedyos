### 1. 🧠 [Mental Attitudes] Quieting Creative Self-Doubt

- **Speaker:** Neal Brennan
- **Timecode:** `01:37 - 02:14`
- **YouTube Deep Link:** [01:37](https://youtu.be/1Z3EzFDVcgo?t=97)
- **Context:** Neal Brennan discusses the severe doubts he faced about his creative ability after Chappelle's Show ended and how he managed his inner critic.

#### Verbatim Quote
> "I had severe doubts about whether I could do it alone. I had a index card in my pocket of funny things that I'd written or done, whether they were sketches on the show or lines or whatever, of things that I'd done as a reminder that I was, had some worth. If I felt like I was drowning or sinking, I could look at this and call off the dogs in my mind."

#### Tactical Takeaway
Keep a written inventory of your best jokes, bits, and past creative achievements to consult when imposter syndrome or self-doubt starts undermining your confidence.
