### 1. 🧠 [Mental Attitudes] Overcoming Intimidation Through Open Mics

- **Speaker:** Joe Rogan
- **Timecode:** `01:11:24 - 01:11:53`
- **YouTube Deep Link:** [01:11:24](https://youtu.be/9yji9vIxkwY?t=4284)
- **Context:** Rogan discusses his early reluctance to try stand-up comedy and how attending his first open mic removed the intimidation factor.

#### Verbatim Quote
> "one of the things about going to an open mic night is you get to see the professionals really the hosts and occasionally professional drop in and do a set but you also get to see these amateurs who are terrible and you go oh I get it so everybody sucks at first and then you know you could just go up with the people who suck and you suck too and it was like okay it was a huge relief because I thought of stand-up oh my god it's like Richard Pryor or Jerry Seinfeld those are bahama gonna be a legend I can't do that"

#### Tactical Takeaway
Recognize that comedy mastery is developed through failure; seeing beginners struggle demystifies the art form and removes the paralyzing expectation that you must be legendary on day one.

### 2. 🎤 [Performance & Stagecraft] Full Mental Immersion During Delivery

- **Speaker:** Joe Rogan
- **Timecode:** `01:13:53 - 01:14:41`
- **YouTube Deep Link:** [01:13:53](https://youtu.be/9yji9vIxkwY?t=4433)
- **Context:** Kevin Smith asks Rogan about the feeling of killing on stage, and Rogan explains why he focuses entirely on internal execution and presence rather than the audience's praise.

#### Verbatim Quote
> "I'm not thinking everybody loves me I'm thinking okay this bit it this here's the peak here's the valley here's where I bring it up and here's where I hammer at home and here comes the pause and then there's the punch and I'm I'm doing I'm going and I'm also in the moment where I have to be when I'm doing a bit if I'm doing a bit on a clock or something like that I have to be thinking about a [ __ ] clock I'm not just saying those words I am thinking 100% about what I'm saying because if I don't it doesn't work as good there's no way you can't just say the words it's a form of hypnosis it's like a mass hypnosis and these people know those [ __ ] animals out there in that crowd they they smell weakness they smell distraction they smell when you're disconnected"

#### Tactical Takeaway
Do not mechanically recite punchlines; actively visualize the subject matter and modulate dynamics, pauses, and peaks in real time, because audiences intuitively sense and reject mental disconnection.

- **Speaker:** Kevin Smith
- **Timecode:** `01:18:02 - 01:18:22`
- **YouTube Deep Link:** [01:18:02](https://youtu.be/9yji9vIxkwY?t=4682)
- **Context:** Smith recalls working with George Carlin during rehearsals and asking about his process for developing new stand-up hours.

#### Verbatim Quote
> "George is like oh I'm working on this bit for the new HBO show he goes it's called advertising lullaby and I said what do you mean working on it and he goes well memorizing it and I was like you memorize your bits and goes yeah I write everything I was like you write your bits I was just thought you kind of came up with [ __ ] off the top your head it goes nuts he's like I write everything and then I have to memorize everything like a script"
